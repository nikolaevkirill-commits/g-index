SkyRhythm public feed contract — v131, 2026-10-19

consumer_cloud/forecast.json and INDEPENDENT_FORECAST_FEED_v1.json contain
source-formula calculations, not resolved consumer verdicts.

Migration: days[date].forecast_result was removed. Its explicit replacement is
days[date].source_formula_result. The score and the source_formula channel retain
their mathematical meaning; no formula, weights, thresholds or expert decisions
were changed. Missing values remain null, never an invented zero.

The application and its notification runtime resolve the final consumer score
using NRConsumerAuthority.resolveFinal: a verified registered expert decision for
the date takes precedence, otherwise a fresh source_formula value is used.
Do not label source_formula_result.score as the final consumer forecast.
For example, on 2026-10-19 the registered expert result is 0 while the formula
snapshot can be -2. Display the resolved 0, not both as competing final scores.

External clients using the removed field must migrate explicitly; do not silently
substitute source_formula_result as a final verdict. This change does not certify
an unknown third-party or native client's behavior.

Frozen research inputs and historical archives are not migrated by this contract.

Compatibility metadata: days[date].consumer_authority describes the source-formula
channel only. It is retained for existing parsers and is NOT the authority of the
resolved final consumer score. New integrations must use resolveFinal for that
verdict. No trust or expert precedence is granted by this JSON metadata.
