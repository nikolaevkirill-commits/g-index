Methodology

Strict 3-class agreement: Engine sign (-1/0/+1) equals expert sign on the same date.

Binary neg/non-neg: Engine and expert both classify day as <0 (negative) or ≥0 (non-negative).

ExactMatch: Engine integer score (-3..+3) equals expert integer score exactly.

Engine output: Discrete integer score on 7-class scale [-3, +3] per day. G = Kp − 2 + ΣAᵢ where ΣAᵢ = Lᵢ + Mᵢ + eᵢ + Pᵢ + Dᵢ.

Ground truth (v5.1): 329 unique dates extracted from 47-page expert PDF bulletin (Jun 2025 → May 2026). 22 textual pages parsed directly + 25 image pages via vision OCR. 12 multi-source inconsistencies reconciled by median rule. Decision-rule sensitivity tested: median/first-issued/last-issued/mode all agree within Δ ≤ 0.7pp.

⚠ Important: what this validation does NOT show. Ground truth is expert PDF bulletins that use the same Vedic Panchanga + Kp framework as our engine. High agreement (κ=0.73 weighted) demonstrates that the engine reliably reproduces expert reasoning. It does not demonstrate that the underlying framework predicts real-world outcomes (medical events, operational incidents, financial movements). Outcome-based validation against independent event records has not been performed. Prospective validation period (90 days from 2026-05-03) is in progress and closes ~2026-08-01. Until then, treat all metrics as in-sample.
