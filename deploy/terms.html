What G-Index does

G-Index combines NOAA space weather data (Kp index, sunspot activity) з Vedic Panchanga calendar calculations to produce a daily composite forecast signal.

Accuracy disclaimer

Internal validation (Jun 2025 – May 2026, n=280):

  • Strict 3-class agreement (negative/neutral/positive day): 71.4% [95% CI 66.1–76.8%]
  • Binary agreement (bad day vs not-bad day): 83.6% [95% CI 79.3–87.9%]
  • Cohen's κ = 0.52 (moderate); quadratic-weighted κ = 0.73 (substantial)
  • Engine outperforms Kp-only baseline (31.1% strict) by +40 percentage points

This means roughly 1 out of 4 predictions can disagree with expert judgement on direction. Use as ONE signal among many — not as sole decision criterion.

⚠ Important caveat about what this validation does NOT show. Our ground truth is expert weekly forecast bulletins that use the same Vedic Panchanga + Kp framework as our engine. High agreement (κ=0.73 weighted) demonstrates that the engine reliably reproduces expert reasoning. It does NOT demonstrate that the framework predicts real-world outcomes such as medical events, operational incidents, or financial movements.

Outcome-based validation against independent event records has not been performed. Prospective validation period (90 days from 2026-05-03) is currently in progress and will close approximately 2026-08-01. Until then, treat all metrics as in-sample.
